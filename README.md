# Retail-Sales-Performance-Analysis
A Power BI Business Analyst portfolio project analysing retail sales performance across products and cities, using DAX measures, interactive dashboards, and data-driven business recommendations.
## Project Overview
This project analyses retail sales performance using Microsoft Power BI. The dashboard presents key performance indicators, including Total Sales, Average Sales, and Number of Sales. It also compares revenue across products and cities to support data-driven business decisions.

## Tools and Technologies

- Microsoft Power BI Desktop
- DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)
- Data Visualisation
- Interactive Dashboards
- Business Performance Analysis
## Key Business Findings

- Total Sales Revenue: £10,000
- Total Sales Transactions: 6
- Average Sales per Transaction: £1,666.67
- Highest-Revenue Product: Laptop (£5,000, 50% of total sales)
- Highest-Revenue City: London (£4,500, 45% of total sales)
## Business Recommendations

- Investigate opportunities to improve Headphones sales.
- Review customer demand and product availability in Manchester.
- Explore targeted marketing strategies to improve sales performance.
- Collect additional sales data before making major business decisions.
 ## Project Limitations

- The dataset contains only six sales transactions.
- Historical sales data was not available for trend analysis.
- Product costs were not included, so profitability could not be assessed.
- Customer information was unavailable for purchasing behaviour analysis.
## Future Project Improvements

- Include historical sales data to analyse monthly sales trends.
- Add product cost information to calculate profitability.
- Include customer data to understand purchasing behaviour.
- Expand the dataset to provide more reliable business insights.
 ## Project Conclusion

This project demonstrates my ability to analyse retail sales data using Microsoft Power BI, create DAX measures, develop interactive dashboards, identify key business insights, and provide data-driven recommendations to support management decisions.
